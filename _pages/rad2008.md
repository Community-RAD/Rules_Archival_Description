The Canadian Committee on Archival Description (CCAD) published the second edition of the Rules for Archival Description (RAD) in 2008. This is the most recent version of RAD, which is managed by the Canadian Council of Archives (CCA).

RAD 2008 is divided into the following sections:

Committees and Working Groups\
[Foreward](https://communityrad.ca/foreword/)\
[Preface to the 2008 Edition](https://communityrad.ca/2008/)\
[Preface to the 1990 Edition](https://communityrad.ca/1990/)\
[Statement of Principles](https://communityrad.ca/principles/)\
[General Introduction](https://communityrad.ca/general-introduction/)

**Part I: Description**\
[Introduction](https://communityrad.ca/introduction/)\
(1). [General Rules for Description](https://communityrad.ca/1-0/)\
(2). [Rules for Description of Units Consisting of Multiple Media](https://communityrad.ca/2-0/)\
(3). [Textual Records](https://communityrad.ca/3-0/)\
(4). [Graphic Material](https://communityrad.ca/4-0/)\
(5). [Cartographic Materials](https://communityrad.ca/5-0/)\
(6). [Architectual and Technical Drawings](https://communityrad.ca/6-0/)\
(7). [Moving Images](https://communityrad.ca/7-0/)\
(8). [Sound Recordings](https://communityrad.ca/8-0/)\
(9). [Records in Electronic Form](https://communityrad.ca/9-0/)\
(10). [Records on Microform](https://communityrad.ca/10-0/)\
(11). [Objects](https://communityrad.ca/11-0/)\
(12). [Philatelic Records](https://communityrad.ca/12-0/)\
(13). [Discrete Items](https://communityrad.ca/13-0/)
